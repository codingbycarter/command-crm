What we will and won't do

We will

  • Explain your reports in plain English
  • Dispute errors and items that can't be verified
  • Give you a written contract with every service and the total cost
  • Charge only after the agreed service is performed
  • Tell you when something is accurate and will stay

We won't

  • Promise a specific score or a guaranteed result
  • Try to remove accurate, current information
  • Ask for payment up front
  • Suggest a "new credit identity" or a CPN
  • Tell you to dispute things you know are correct

Your rights under federal law

A summary of the Credit Repair Organizations Act. You'll get the full required statement, “Consumer Credit File Rights Under State and Federal Law,” before you sign anything.

  • You can get a free copy of your credit report from each of the three bureaus at annualcreditreport.com.
  • A credit repair company can't charge you until it has fully performed the services it promised.
  • You must receive a written, dated contract before any work begins.
  • You can cancel the contract for any reason, without penalty, within 3 business days of signing it.
  • No one can legally remove accurate and timely negative information from your credit report.
  • You can sue a credit repair company that violates this law.
You can do this yourself. You have the right to dispute inaccurate information directly with the credit bureaus and creditors at no cost. We're here if you'd rather have someone handle it with you.
