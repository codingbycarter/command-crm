Cold-Call Scripts & Sales Strategy

Script A

Business without a website

“Hi! My name is Brittany. I've got some good news and some bad news. Which would you like first?

The bad news is, this is a cold call.

The good news is, I came across your business and couldn't find a website, so I put together a quick preview of what one could look like.

Could I have 60 seconds to show you?

If you like it, I can customize and launch it for you, with starter packages beginning at $500. If it's not something you need, no pressure.”

Only say you've created a preview when one actually exists.
Script B

Business with a website

“Hi, my name is Brittany. I help cleaning, HVAC and plumbing businesses save time handling incoming customer inquiries.

Quick question: when you're out on a job and a potential customer calls, who normally answers?

I offer virtual front desk solutions that can help capture inquiries and appointment requests while you're busy.

Would you be open to a quick demonstration to see whether something like that could help your business?”

Script C

Offering the bundle

“Along with the website, I can also help you set up a virtual front desk so customers have a way to reach your business even when you're unavailable.

We can start with the website alone, or I can prepare a package that includes both services.

Which would be more useful for your business right now?”

Common objections

“I can build a website myself.”

“Absolutely. My service is for business owners who would rather spend their time running their business than designing, launching and maintaining a website.”

“That's too expensive.”

“I understand. We can look at a smaller package that focuses on your most important needs rather than paying for features you won't use.”

“I'm not interested.”

“No problem. Thanks for your time, and I wish you continued success with your business.”

“Send me some information.”

“Of course. What's the best business email address? I'll send a short overview and a preview you can review when convenient.”
